1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FriendsZone Chat City (“FriendsZone,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the website and its chat features. Please read it before creating an account or using the service.

1.1 Information we may collect

  • Account information: information used to register and sign in, such as your email address and authentication details handled by Firebase Authentication. We do not ask you to share your password with other users.
  • Profile information: information you choose or provide for your profile, such as your name, username, profile picture, and other profile fields made available in the service.

  • Friend and group information: friend requests, friend connections, group membership, group names, and related actions.
