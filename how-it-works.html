How Friends Zone Chat City Works

Getting started takes only a few minutes. Here is how to make the most of the site.

1. Create your account

Sign up with your email and complete your profile with your name, age and nationality. A complete profile helps other members know who you are.

2. Start chatting

Once you are signed in, you can send messages and share photos in the chat. Say hello, join the conversation and meet people from different places.

3. Add friends

Found someone you like talking to? Send a friend request. When they accept, you will be on each other's friends list and it becomes easier to stay in touch. You can also like a member's profile to show you enjoy their company.

4. Join a live room

Live rooms are shared spaces where a whole group chats together at the same time. Open a live room, see who is online and join in.

5. Make voice and video calls

Want to talk instead of type? You can start a voice or video call with your friends right from the site.

6. Earn ranks

The more you take part, the higher your rank grows. Ranks are a fun way to show how active you are in the community.

7. Install it on your phone

You can add Friends Zone Chat City to your home screen so it opens like an app. In your browser menu, choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.

Stay safe

Never share your password, and be careful about sharing personal details with people you have just met. Read our Privacy Policy & Terms to learn how your information is handled.
